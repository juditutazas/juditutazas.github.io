Find Juditutazas in San Diego, CA

Juditutazas sits in San Diego, CA, close to everyday foot traffic. Ask the desk about parking and the easiest entrance when you arrive.

Before you visit

  • We're at 350 Fifth Avenue, San Diego, CA. Holiday weeks may shift hours — this site updates before social posts.
  • Groups of six or more should message ahead so we can seat you together.
  • We're a short walk from the main transit stop in the area.
  • The side entrance is wheelchair-friendly — ask us to meet you there.
  • Street parking is easiest before 10am on weekdays.
