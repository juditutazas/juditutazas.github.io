FAQ

Questions people ask before bringing in an advisor.

What kind of consulting does Juditutazas do?

We focus on strategy, operations, pricing, facilitation and implementation support for growing teams.

Do you work remotely or in person?

Both. This upgraded site presents Juditutazas as San Diego-based with remote and on-site engagement options.

What size companies do you work with?

Usually founder-led, small-to-mid-size teams and organizations in transition or growth.

Do you only advise, or do you help implement too?

Implementation support is part of the offer. The goal is not just a deck, but movement.

How do engagements start?

Usually with a discovery call, followed by a scoped recommendation or workshop plan.

Can you facilitate offsites or leadership sessions?

Yes. Workshops, team planning and decision-making sessions are built into the service model.
